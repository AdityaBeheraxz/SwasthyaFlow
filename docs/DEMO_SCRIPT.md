# Five-minute judge demo

1. **0:00–0:30** Open the landing page. Explain the frontline information problem.
2. **0:30–1:15** Select Health Worker, open New intake, record consent, choose Hindi or Odia, and review the original text.
3. **1:15–2:00** Add the synthetic CBC report text and generate a triage note.
4. **2:00–2:45** Open the triage card; inspect sources, missing information, questions, and rule explanation.
5. **2:45–3:30** Switch to Medical Officer, edit the summary, then approve or escalate.
6. **3:30–4:00** Prepare and save a referral, then print.
7. **4:00–4:30** Open Audit & privacy to show consent and review events.
8. **4:30–5:00** Close: AI organises information; qualified staff make clinical decisions.
